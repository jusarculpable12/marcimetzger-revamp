## Direction

Chosen: `name-as-monument`, seed `1691123569`. No redraws.

Why: Marci’s own name is the strongest brand asset. Setting it as a landscape-sized boundary sign makes the page unmistakably hers, while the valley photograph supplies the place competitors cannot borrow.

Caps applied: none; the final inspector reported zero failures at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes.

Top three remaining problems:

1. The available gallery assets are broader real-estate imagery rather than a feed of Marci’s current properties; a native MLS/IDX connection would make this section genuinely live.
2. There is no verified local footage of Marci or Pahrump, so the hero uses an honest slow photographic drift rather than generic stock video.
3. The mobile footer includes one intentional long visual rest before the closing wordmark; it is deliberate, but it is the first place to compress if a denser mobile page is preferred.

## Werner Blumberg reference redesign

Direction: `uneven-photographs`, seed `1628515226`. No redraws. The existing olive-and-bone palette and Bodoni/Jost typography were kept.

Reference principles carried over: restrained navigation over a full architectural image; a centred serif hero; a dark image-led service portal; generous editorial whitespace; uneven photographic placement; and information docked against large photographs. The reference brand, copy, exact layout, and New York visual identity were not copied.

Idea: On this page, Marci’s Mountain Falls landscape becomes a quiet portfolio of ways to enter Pahrump.

Live thing: The complete property search remains the page’s working centre, with direct access to Marci’s current Zillow profile.

Final critique, round 1: Seen in one second, this is a polished Pahrump real-estate site led by Marci’s local landscape. The desktop hero and three service portals carry the reference rhythm clearly; the mobile hero and menu remain direct and readable. Design 8.7 · usability 8.8 · creativity 8.2 · content 9.0 → overall 8.7. No caps applied. The remaining limitation is content rather than composition: a true IDX feed would let the editorial listing treatment use current property inventory instead of routing to Zillow.
